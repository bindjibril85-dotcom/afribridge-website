COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know
before we begin.

A few basics about our role and how we work.

Does AfriBridge manufacture or supply products directly?＋

No. AfriBridge primarily facilitates business connections and sourcing discussions. The supplier and buyer should verify specifications, terms and responsibilities directly.

Can you help find an Indian manufacturer?＋

Yes. Share the product or service requirements, quantity, destination, expected timeline and budget range so we can assess the request.

Do you guarantee a supplier or project outcome?＋

No. Research and introductions do not guarantee supplier quality, project award, shipment, payment or commercial success. Independent due diligence is essential.

How does AfriBridge charge for its services?＋

Any research fee, coordination fee or success-based commission should be agreed in writing before work begins or introductions are made.

Can I contact you directly on WhatsApp?＋

Yes. Use the WhatsApp contact button or send a message through the contact form below.
